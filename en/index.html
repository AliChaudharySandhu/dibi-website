Decarbonisation Pathways

We create digital twins of bus depots with Australian software and a routing-energy model to plan a cost-effective, scalable shift to electric buses—cutting emissions and supporting Indonesia’s battery value chain.

Renewables Integration

We model solar, storage and grid options and optimise depot charging, while assessing building efficiency and thermal comfort to cut pollutants (PM2.5, PM10, carbon, nitrates).

Equity & Inclusion

Using GEDSI-led surveys, FGDs and site observations, we examine who benefits and how. Early results show women are ~77% of users and many report heat stress—evidence guiding more inclusive, healthy public transport.

KONEKSI – Australia-Indonesia Knowledge Partnership Platform

Partnerships for inclusive and sustainable policies and technologies.

KONEKSI supports equitable knowledge partnerships between Australian and Indonesian organisations, leveraging local knowledge to address socio-economic challenges. It engages diverse stakeholders from academia, government, civil society, and the private sector to co-design multidisciplinary solutions.

This collaborative initiative is funded by the Australian Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT) through the KONEKSI Knowledge Platform that supports partnerships between Australian and Indonesian organisations for inclusive and sustainable policy and technology.

Research Spotlight: DIBI
